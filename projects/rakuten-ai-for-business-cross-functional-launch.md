# Rakuten AI for Business - Cross-Functional Launch

> **Document type:** Externally grounded interview case reconstruction, not a claim of an independently verified completed engagement.
>
> **Timeline alignment:** The [public AI Technical Program Management resume](https://github.com/beastofbayarea/shivam-singh-ai-tpm/blob/main/shivam-singh-ai-tpm.pdf) is used only to place this case within the Rakuten Group role dated June 2023-December 2023.

## Evidence-grounded premise

Rakuten announced in August 2023 that it would explore conversational AI experiences for consumers and businesses while improving productivity for merchants and business partners. In November 2023, Rakuten unveiled Rakuten AI for Business as an invitation-only platform spanning marketing, sales, customer support, operations, strategy planning, and engineering. NIST's AI Risk Management Framework organizes responsible AI work through Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage functions. Together, these sources support a cross-functional launch program built around explicit ownership, risk evidence, staged rollout, and operational readiness.

## Case approach

- Establish coordinated workstreams for product experience, data, evaluation, privacy and security, merchant enablement, support, and operations.
- Build a dependency-based work-back plan with accountable owners, RAID governance, decision dates, and launch-readiness gates.
- Translate intended business workflows into evaluation scenarios, acceptance thresholds, escalation paths, and rollback criteria.
- Release to bounded merchant cohorts, review evidence at each gate, and expand only when safety, reliability, usability, and support readiness meet the agreed criteria.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use critical-dependency closure, readiness-gate acceptance, evaluation pass rate, unsupported-response rate, latency, incident recovery, time to first completed workflow, eligible-merchant adoption, and support demand. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
