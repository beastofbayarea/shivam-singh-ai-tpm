# Cloud Partnership Recovery — Incident Response & Vendor Governance

## What I worked on

I completed this work during my [Microsoft experience from January 2020 to August 2022](https://github.com/beastofbayarea/shivam-singh-ai-tpm/blob/main/shivam-singh-ai-tpm.pdf).

I recovered a $10M annual cloud relationship supporting 20 startups and roughly one million users. Technical stabilization and commercial accountability ran as parallel workstreams, supported by independent telemetry, impact-led prioritization, realistic load tests, and a permanent vendor scorecard.

## At a glance

- I led recovery across 20 startups after outages, delayed APIs, and weak provider reporting caused quantified financial and engagement harm.
- I restored 99.9% uptime and delivered three blocked APIs within 30 days through a phased technical plan and realistic portfolio stress tests.
- I renegotiated the cloud agreement by 15%, saving $1.5M annually, and replaced provider-authored reporting with independent performance governance.

## The situation

The portfolio relied on a vendor's summaries while load balancing, database capacity, autoscaling, and roadmap commitments deteriorated. One fintech lost $500K in two hours, and another company saw engagement fall 15%.

## What I needed to accomplish

I needed to restore critical services quickly while rebuilding the vendor operating model so performance failures had observable owners, dated commitments, and commercial consequences.

## What I did

- I created a cross-functional incident team and prioritized by customer loss, regulatory dependency, data integrity, and shared platform exposure.
- I reconstructed an independent incident timeline from startup reports, service logs, configurations, and capacity metrics.
- I phased load-balancer, autoscaling, database, API, redundancy, and anomaly-detection work with explicit exit evidence.
- I escalated verified SLA breaches to provider executives and tied future review, remedies, and renewal to a shared scorecard.

## The results

- Operational uptime returned to 99.9%.
- All three delayed APIs shipped within 30 days.
- The contract concession saved $1.5M per year.
- The relationship moved from trust-based summaries to observable service governance.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- I treated the failure as both an incident and a vendor-governance problem.
- I tested fixes against market spikes, flash sales, and streaming loads before closure.
- I tied commercial remedies to independently verified performance.

## How I led

I kept startup teams informed through concise daily and weekly updates while coordinating engineers, security specialists, vendor executives, and portfolio leadership around one recovery plan.

## Why I chose this approach

I used [Basel Committee - Principles for operational resilience (2021)](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d516.pdf) to ground operational-resilience and recovery framework. I used [Google SRE - Monitoring Distributed Systems](https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/) to ground monitoring and actionable-alert methodology.
