## Evidence-grounded premise

The World Bank's 2014 PPP Reference Guide emphasizes institutional frameworks, risk allocation, development stages, and implementation governance. The Agile Manifesto prioritizes collaboration, working increments, and adapting to evidence. Applied to APAC market entry, those sources support a program that makes local responsibilities, risks, dependencies, and decision gates explicit.

## Case approach

- Decompose entry into regulatory, commercial, product, partner, technology, and operating-model workstreams.
- Allocate each material risk to the party best able to manage it and define escalation rights.
- Use market-specific pilots and working increments before committing to full rollout.
- Maintain one integrated dependency map, evidence log, decision cadence, and exit criteria.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use milestone evidence, unresolved dependencies, risk aging, partner readiness, pilot outcomes, decision latency, and controlled-exit performance. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
