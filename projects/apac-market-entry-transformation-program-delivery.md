# APAC Market Entry - Transformation Program Delivery

> **Portfolio lens:** Technical program management, data-platform modernization, launch governance, regional dependencies, regulatory delivery, and cross-functional execution.

## Executive snapshot

Structured and delivered a two-year transformation supporting a $1.1B APAC opportunity. A governed analytics platform became the decision system for a hub-and-spoke market-entry program, with explicit workstream ownership, regional decision rights, compliance gates, and localized GTM execution.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led transformation from three-week reporting lag to sub-15-minute decision data while reducing total cost of ownership 40%.
- Defined platform ownership, data stewardship, certified metrics, regional challenge rights, and a hub-and-spoke delivery model for market entry.
- Improved conversion from 4% to 22%, reduced CAC payback from 18 to nine months, shortened compliance review 30%, and enabled $1.1B in APAC AUM.

## Interview story

### Situation

The market-entry program relied on fragmented reports, unowned data quality, and a copied launch playbook that converted only 8% in Asia.

### Task

Convert the market-entry strategy into an executable transformation program spanning technology, analytics, regulation, marketing, sales, finance, and regional operations.

### Actions

- Established explicit ownership for platform reliability, data quality, certified metrics, and regional decisions.
- Used a single real-time launch dashboard as the first delivery milestone to prove value before funding the full lakehouse.
- Coordinated local experts and data-science teams to identify regional bias and recalibrate the global scoring model by market.
- Sequenced a Singapore governance hub with local data and GTM spokes in Vietnam and Indonesia.
- Turned regulatory constraints into repeatable data, approval, messaging, and launch requirements.

### Results

- Data latency fell below 15 minutes, and total cost of ownership declined 40%.
- Conversion reached 22%.
- CAC payback fell to nine months, and compliance review time improved 30%.
- The program supported $1.1B in APAC AUM.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Establish accountable data ownership before scaling market activity.
- Keep primary regulated data in-country while returning anonymized insight to the regional hub.
- Prove the program through one decision-critical release before expanding the platform.

## Leadership signal

Aligned the CFO, CIO, CMO, technology, analytics, marketing, sales, compliance, finance, and regional leaders around one market-entry scorecard, delivery model, and set of launch gates.
